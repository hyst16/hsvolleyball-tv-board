How to save edits (no auth required):
1) Click "Apply changes" to update the preview.
2) Click "Copy JSON" (or Download).
3) Click "Edit on GitHub" to open teams.json in the repo, paste the updated JSON, and commit — that saves your edits to the main branch.
To delete an entire office key: select it from the dropdown and click "Delete selected office". Changes are local to this page until you paste/commit on GitHub.
